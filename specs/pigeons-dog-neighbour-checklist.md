# Manual checklist: pigeons, dog and warning icon (W4 Part 1)

Play at https://pinzzas.github.io/stroller/ (no neighbour yet; that is Part 2).
First check the build label (bottom right) shows the newest build. If not, refresh.
Keyboard: Up = faster, Down = slower, S = Shush. iPhone: tap `Faster`, `Slower`, `Shush`.
Obstacles are random each load. To find one, keep walking (handle other obstacles as usual) or reload the page. Each kind shows up about once every 40 s.

1. **Spot every kind.** Walk for about 2 minutes. You should see potholes, crossings, three small blue-grey boxes at the back of the pavement (pigeons) and a wooden fence. They are easy to tell apart. No green neighbour should appear.
2. **Pigeons, right and wrong.**
   - Right: when pigeons come, press Down to `Speed: slow` before they reach the stroller and stay slow. They stay put, no pink flash, no drop.
   - Wrong: next pigeons, stay at normal. They fly up off the top of the screen, pink flash, meter drops by 10 (once).
   - Halfway: go slow, then press Up while the front wheel is among them. They fly off, drop by 10, once.
3. **Dog warning.** At normal, watch the top right. An amber box saying `woof?` appears above the roofs before any fence is on screen. A few seconds later a wooden fence slides in.
4. **Dog, right.** When `woof?` appears, press Up to `Speed: brisk` and stay brisk past the whole fence. No dog, no flash, no drop. `woof?` goes away once the front of the stroller is past the end of the fence.
5. **Dog, wrong.** Next `woof?`, stay at normal. While you pass the fence a brown box pops up with `WOOF!` for about 1.5 s. Pink flash, meter drops by 25 (once), and `woof?` disappears at once. Also try going brisk, then pressing Down while still along the fence: it barks, drop by 25.
   - Known quirk, expected: if two dogs come in a row, `woof?` stays on after the first fence because the next dog is already in range.
6. **Restart and try to break it.** Mash Up, Down and S together, change speed in the middle of pigeons and fences, then let the baby wake and press Enter (or tap `Walk again`). The game should not freeze or show odd labels. The new walk shows no `woof?`, no dog and no flying pigeons left over.
