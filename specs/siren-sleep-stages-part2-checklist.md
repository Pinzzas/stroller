# Manual checklist: the siren and "one at a time" (W5 Part 2)

Play at https://pinzzas.github.io/stroller/. First check the build label (bottom right) shows the newest build. If not, refresh.
Keyboard: Up = faster, Down = slower, S = Shush. iPhone: tap `Faster`, `Slower`, `Shush`.
A phone stopwatch helps. The siren comes late (about 1:30 at normal speed), so play carefully and keep a Shush for it. Going brisk (Up) brings it sooner.

1. **Potholes first.** Start a walk. The first 3 obstacles are all potholes (dark brown holes). The 4th, at about 0:25 at normal speed, is a crossing (zebra road). No pigeons, dog, neighbour or siren yet.
2. **One new kind at a time.** Keep walking at normal speed. Pigeons first show as the 7th obstacle (about 0:40). The dog is the 10th (about 1:00, `woof?` first). The neighbour is the 13th (about 1:15). No kind appears before its turn. Every new kind is the first obstacle of its block of 3.
3. **No siren on the street.** The siren is the 16th obstacle (about 1:30). Nothing is drawn on the road for it, and it must not look like a pothole. Instead an amber `nee-naw` icon appears top right (where `woof?` shows), with a thin bar under it that fills up as you get closer.
4. **Siren, done right.** Keep at least one Shush. When the bar is full and the icon turns coral, press S (or tap `Shush`). You should see `Shhh!`, the meter goes up by 10, the Shush label drops by one, and the icon and bar vanish. No hit, no pink flash.
5. **Siren, done wrong.** Next siren: don't press S. `NEE-NAW!` shows top right where the icon was, for about 1.5 s, with a pink flash and a loud-noise drop (25 in Light, 20 in Deep, 15 in Dreamy). Also try a siren with no Shush left (use both first): pressing S at the coral peak does nothing, and the siren hits. Pressing S too early (bar half full) is a normal Shush (+10) but the siren still hits.
6. **Walk again.** Let the baby wake: the siren icon, bar and `NEE-NAW!` are hidden on the end screen. Press Enter (or tap `Walk again`). The new walk starts with 3 potholes again, and nothing from the last walk is left on screen.
7. **Try to break it.** Mash S, Up and Down together as a siren arrives. Slow down or speed up inside the peak. Stop to chat with a neighbour while the `nee-naw` icon is showing (the siren should wait until you move again). Nothing should freeze, flicker, or hit twice for one siren.
8. **Touch.** On iPhone Safari (landscape) repeat step 4 with the `Shush` button: tapping it at the coral peak calms the siren, as on the keyboard.
