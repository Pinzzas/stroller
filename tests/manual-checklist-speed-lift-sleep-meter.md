# Manual checklist: speed, lift and sleep meter (W2, task 1)

Game link: https://pinzzas.github.io/stroller/
Check the build label (bottom-right) shows the newest commit before you start.

## A. Computer browser

1. Open the game link. At the top centre you see a white box with a full
   lavender bar and the text `Sleep 100`. Top-left says `Speed: normal`. Three
   see-through buttons sit along the bottom (`Slower`, `Faster`, `Lift (hold)`).
2. Press Down once: the label says `Speed: slow` and the street slows. Press
   Down again: nothing changes. Press Up twice: `Speed: brisk` and the street is
   clearly faster. Press Up again: nothing changes. Hold Up: it does not skip
   steps. Arrow keys and Space must not scroll the page.
3. Hold Space: the stroller tilts back at once (front end up). Keep holding: in
   under a second it drops by itself. Let go, press again: it tilts again.
4. Wait for the small dark-grey bump (within about 8 seconds). Do nothing as it
   reaches the right-hand wheel: the stroller flashes pink and the meter shows
   `Sleep 90`. About 3 seconds later it creeps up about 1 a second. Hit another
   bump, then press Down: once it starts rising, it rises about 3 a second.
5. Hold Space just before the next bump reaches the front wheel: it passes
   under with no flash and the meter stays put.
6. Click `Faster`, `Slower` and press-and-hold `Lift (hold)` with the mouse:
   same as the keys.
7. Try to break it: switch to brisk and let bumps hit until `Sleep 0`. The bar
   is empty, the walk carries on, the meter refills later, and each bump costs
   only 10. Also hold Space, switch to another tab, release Space there, come
   back: the stroller is not stuck tilted.

## B. iPhone Safari (landscape)

8. Open the game link. The page loads, the meter and `Speed:` label are easy to
   read, the buttons are not hidden under the rounded corners or home bar.
9. Tap `Faster` and `Slower`: the label and street speed change. Tap fast many
   times: the page never zooms and no text gets selected.
10. Hold `Lift (hold)`: the stroller tilts. Lifting over a bump avoids the hit;
    missing it drops the meter by 10.
11. Hold `Lift (hold)` with one thumb and tap `Faster` with the other: both work
    at once.
12. Try to break it: long-press every button. No copy/select menu or magnifier
    appears. Switch to another app for 10 seconds and back: the walk carries
    on with no big jump and the stroller is not stuck tilted.
