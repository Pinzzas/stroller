# Manual checklist: chatty neighbour (Part 2)

Play at https://pinzzas.github.io/stroller/ . First check the build label (bottom right) is the new one. Obstacles come in random order, so let other ones pass until the one you want. Before each step, Shush or wait until the meter is near Sleep 100.

1. **Walk past (keyboard).** When you see the tall green box at the back of the pavement, press Up until `Speed: brisk` before the stroller reaches it. You should walk straight past. No speech bubble, no drop in the meter.
2. **Get stopped.** Next neighbour: stay at normal. When the stroller reaches them the street should stop and a white bubble should say `Ooh, is the baby asleep? So...`. The meter should fall slowly (about 3 a second). After about 5 seconds the bubble goes, the walk carries on, and the meter is about 15 lower.
3. **Phone or touch.** Tap `Faster` until `Speed: brisk` for a neighbour: you should walk past. Get stopped once, then tap `Shush` during the chat: `Shhh!` shows and the meter goes up by about 10.
4. **Waking during a chat.** Let the meter get low (under 15), then get stopped by a neighbour. The baby wakes during the chat: the bubble should disappear and the usual end box should appear. Press Enter or `Walk again`: the new walk has no bubble.
5. **Quick pigeons and dog check.** Pigeons at slow: they stay put. Pigeons at normal: they fly off, meter drops 10. When `woof?` shows, go brisk past the fence: no bark. At normal: `WOOF!`, meter drops 25. Both should still work as before.
6. **Try to break it.** While stopped by a neighbour, hammer Up, Down, Space and S all at once (or the buttons on touch). The street should stay still until the chat ends, then move at the speed on the label. Nothing should freeze, and the meter should not refill during the chat.
