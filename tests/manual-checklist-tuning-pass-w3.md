# Manual checklist: tuning pass after week 3

Play at https://pinzzas.github.io/stroller/ in a computer browser.
First check the build label (bottom right) shows the newest build. If not, refresh.

1. **Do nothing.** Load the page and press nothing. The baby should wake in about 30 s (at most about 70 s). The end box shows "Asleep for" under 1:10.
2. **More to handle.** Play at normal speed. An obstacle should come about every 6 seconds, and about 2 in 5 should be crossings.
3. **Hits stick.** Let one pothole hit (Sleep 90), then lift cleanly. For about 5 seconds the meter does not move. Then it creeps up about 1 point every 4 seconds (the label ticks up once right when the 5 seconds end, because it rounds up). Press Down to slow: about 3 points every 4 seconds.
4. **Lift and Shush.** Hold Space: the stroller drops by itself a little sooner than before. Lifting just before a pothole still clears it at every speed. After a hit, press S: the meter goes up by 10. Use both shushes: the label shows "Shush: back in 45s", counts down, then "Shush: 2 left".
5. **Never slow down.** Stay at normal and lift for everything, but never slow for crossings. The baby should wake in about a minute. Then play again, slowing for every crossing and lifting every time: it should last clearly longer, usually 3 minutes or more.
6. **Try to break it.** Hold Space the whole time, mash Up/Down and S together, and press Enter right as the baby wakes. The game should not freeze, glitch, or show a wrong label.
7. **Gate: 10 runs.** Play 10 walks your natural way and write down each "Asleep for" time. How many ended under 1 min, 1-3 min, 3-5 min, over 5 min? Did each wake-up feel like your own fault? Was it fun, did you want one more walk? You decide pass or fail. Good sign: most runs end between 1 and 5 minutes and feel fair.
