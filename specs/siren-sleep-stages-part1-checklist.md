# Manual checklist: sleep stages and busier street (W5 Part 1)

Play at https://pinzzas.github.io/stroller/ (no siren yet; that is Part 2).
First check the build label (bottom right) shows the newest build. If not, refresh.
Keyboard: Up = faster, Down = slower, S = Shush. iPhone: tap `Faster`, `Slower`, `Shush`.
A phone stopwatch helps. To last 2+ minutes, play carefully and use Shush.

1. **Label.** Start a walk. Under the Shush label (top left) you should see `Light sleep`.
2. **Stages change.** Keep walking. At about 1:00 it should say `Deep sleep`, and at about 2:00 `Dreamy sleep`. It stays `Dreamy sleep` after that, even after you get hit.
3. **Hits cost less.** Miss a pothole in each stage (let the meter settle first so you can read the drop). Light: -10. Deep: -8. Dreamy: -6. A dog bark in Dreamy: -15 (it was -25 in Light).
4. **Busier street.** At normal speed, count obstacles passing the stroller over 30 s at the start (about 5). Count again after 2:30 (about 9). The second count should be clearly higher. Tell us if Dreamy feels rushed.
5. **Looks and behaves as before.** In Light sleep: potholes are dark brown holes, crossings have a zebra road, pigeons are three small blue-grey boxes, the dog is a wooden fence with `woof?` first, the neighbour is a green box that stops you and chats. Each one still hurts or is avoided the same way as before. (Drawing code moved to a new file, so look for missing, misplaced or wrong-coloured things.) Obstacles already on screen should never jump or slide when the stage changes.
6. **Restart and try to break it.** Mash Up, Down and S together while the stage changes, and change speed mid-fence or mid-pigeons. Let the baby wake: the label is hidden on the end screen. Press Enter (or tap `Walk again`). You should see `Light sleep` again and a quiet street, with nothing left over from the last walk. Nothing should freeze or flicker.
7. **Touch.** On iPhone Safari (landscape) repeat steps 1 and 2 with the buttons. The label shows and changes as on the keyboard.
