# Manual checklist: Shush with cooldown

Play at https://pinzzas.github.io/stroller/ (reload the page first so you get the newest version).

## On a computer browser

1. Load the page. Top left you should see `Speed: normal`, and right under it `Shush: 2 left`.
2. Sleep is at 100. Press S once. You should see `Shhh!` above the stroller for about a second, the meter staying at `Sleep 100`, and the label changing to `Shush: 1 left`. (Using it at full sleep wastes a charge on purpose.) Now reload the page to get both charges back.
3. Wait for a pothole hit (`Sleep 90`) and press S straight away. The meter should jump to 100, `Shhh!` shows for about a second, and the label says `Shush: 1 left`.
4. Wait until sleep is back to 100, then let a crossing hit both curbs (`Sleep 60`) and press S. The meter should show `Sleep 75` and the label `Shush: back in 30s`, counting down about once a second.
5. Try to break it: during the countdown, press S many times and hold S down. Nothing should happen: no `Shhh!`, no sleep gain. After about 30 seconds the label returns to `Shush: 2 left` and S works again. Also hold S for a few seconds when you have 2 charges: it should use only one charge.

## On iPhone Safari (landscape)

6. A see-through `Shush` button sits just left of `Lift (hold)`. After a jolt, tap it: the meter goes up by 15, `Shhh!` shows, and the label changes, just like S. Hold `Lift (hold)` with one thumb while tapping `Shush` with the other: both should work together.
